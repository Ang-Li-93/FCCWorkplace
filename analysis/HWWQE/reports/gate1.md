### Higgs frame from the recoil (truth level, channel A)

With a perfect Z reconstruction, the recoil against the Z system targets exactly the W⁺W⁻ frame
(momentum conservation); what remains is the unmeasured initial state:
- **Beam-energy spread** (σ(√s′) = 0.31 GeV) sets the core: about 1–2 mrad on the analyser directions.
- **ISR** (mean photon energy 1.8 GeV; 12.7 % of events above 5 GeV) sets the tails: 8 % of events have
  |Δcosθ| > 0.05; RMS angular error 89 mrad, 67 mrad inside the plan's recoil-mass window 120–140 GeV.
- **Effect on the tomography:** coefficient shifts ≤ 0.13σ of the data statistical error, ≤ 0.1σ on
  I₃, 𝒞₂, h₄₄. This belongs in the M\*-binned response; the plan's ISR kinematic fit removes most of the tail.

### Dedicated samples (Sec. 5.3), channel A

- **WHIZARD's isotropic/diagonal decay switches cannot be used**: they act only on on-shell
  factorised decays, and an H → W⁺W⁻ chain with both W on shell is closed at m_H = 125 GeV
  (tested: WHIZARD stops). **Route used instead:** WHIZARD generates e⁺e⁻ → ℓℓH with the full
  H → ℓνqq′ matrix element (off-shell W\*, spin correlations exact); then each W's decay direction
  is redrawn in its rest frame (`iso`: exactly 𝟙/9; `diag`: decohered SM; `copy`: SM = S1); then
  Pythia8 + Delphes IDEA. The W momenta and the m_W\* spectrum are untouched.
- **Fix found by the cross-check:** Pythia8 lets the W bosons radiate photons by default; it is now
  switched off (`TimeShower:QEDshowerByOther = off`, Pythia 8.310 documentation), matching the
  central sample. 'on' becomes a systematic variation.
- **≤ 1k-event end-to-end tests (2026-10-16), μμ and ee cards:**

  | Sample | target | χ²/80 | p (pseudo-experiments) |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | μμ / ee, copy (S1) | SM | 96.8 / 83.0 | 0.35 / 0.67 |
  | μμ / ee, iso (S2) | 𝟙/9 | 73.4 / 77.4 | 0.87 / 0.77 |
  | μμ / ee, diag (S3) | decohered SM | 73.8 / 93.6 | 0.89 / 0.38 |

  S2 null: I₃ at fixed SM settings −0.03 ± 0.40 (μμ), 0.25 ± 0.34 (ee). S2 reweighted to the SM vs S1:
  p = 0.83 (weights ≤ 3.3, 57 % effective events). In all 6000 events W = analyser + partner and no
  W radiates. M\* < 12 GeV present (5.8 ± 0.7 %, 6.5 ± 0.8 %): the dedicated samples model the region
  the central sample lacks. Larger generator-level tests (4000 events) gave p = 0.57–0.93 for all checks.
- **Cost (measured):** about 200 s per 1000-event job, of which about 170 s is WHIZARD's fixed setup;
  8.4 MB per 1000 events. Channel A at 10× data (94k events): about 1.1 CPU-h and 0.8 GB per sample type.
  S2 is better at ≥ 25× (≈ 240k events, ≈ 3 CPU-h, ≈ 2 GB), since reweighting to the SM keeps about 57 % of
  the events effectively.
- **S4** (6-fermion continuum ℓℓℓνqq′): works, but integration dominates: ≈ 250–500 CPU-h for the 8
  channel-A cards. The one tested component is ≈ 1 % of the signal in the plan's windows; the ee
  cards and the interference were not tested.

## Deviations from PLAN.md and why

1. **Stage1 environment:** the winter2023 stack (`setup_hbs.sh`, key4hep 2024-03-10) instead of
   the current stable release (Sec. 2): the flavour tagger and the EDM4hep-1 reading of winter2023
   files only work there, and the current FCCAnalyses build mis-evaluates `abs(double)` (Gate 0).
   qtomo stays on key4hep 2026-04-08.
2. **Truth H frame = W⁺W⁻ rest frame**, not the recorded H (generator facts above).
3. **Fiducial region M\* ≥ 12 GeV** for anything using the central sample; at reco level I propose
   M\*_reco > 15 GeV so the generator floor never matters.
4. **Dedicated samples:** the redraw-before-shower route instead of WHIZARD's isotropic-decay
   switch (which does not work for an off-shell W\*); Pythia8 shower instead of Pythia6 (WHIZARD
   3.1.5 cannot shower edited events); WHIZARD 3.1.5 and the 2024-03-10 reconstruction stack
   instead of the central 3.0.3 / 2022-12-23. S1 (copy) is recommended alongside S2 as the
   like-for-like Pythia8 reference, even though the central signal passed.
5. **Scope:** channel A only (Ang's decision); channel C's truth validation done as well.
6. **S4 deferred** until a Phase 2 selection exists.

### Draft note to the FCC MC contact (for Ang)

> Subject: winter2023 ZH samples (WHIZARD + Pythia6): Higgs record entry and off-shell boson mass floor
>
> Two features of the winter2023 IDEA ZH samples in which Pythia6 decays the Higgs, found while
> validating H → WW\* spin correlations (wzp6_ee_{ee,mumu,qq,ss,cc,bb}H_HWW_ecm240):
>
> 1. The PDG-25 entry whose daughters are the Higgs decay products carries the pre-shower WHIZARD
>    momentum. Showering the Z decay products raises the invariant mass of the Z system, and the
>    (Z system, H) pair is rescaled along its common axis, so the Higgs that actually decays has less
>    momentum: in Z → qq samples in 99 % of events (median 1.6 GeV, 90 % 6 GeV), in Z → ℓℓ samples in
>    about 20 % (mostly below 1 GeV). Its mass and direction are unchanged. The Higgs four-momentum
>    should be built from its decay products.
> 2. Pythia6's default CKIN(45) = CKIN(47) = 12 GeV removes events with an off-shell W\* below 12 GeV
>    (about 5.8 % of H → WW\*), while the procDict σ × BR uses the inclusive branching ratio. H → ZZ\* is
>    presumably affected in the same way (not checked).
