This is the paper's fit: "Background processes (WW, ZZ, Z/gamma* and rare processes) are modeled as separate components, each with a normalization uncertainty of 1% included as a nuisance parameter in the fit"; "Shape uncertainties are not included as nuisance parameters in this study" (Sec. 5.3).

- **`xsec` (nothing frozen)**: BES, the lepton / jet momentum scale and sqrt(s) profiled in addition - beyond the paper's quoted precision. The paper evaluated these effects separately by repeating the fit with shifted inputs ("The combined uncertainty from these effects is approximately 0.095%, dominated by the BES contribution", Sec. 5.3); the reproduction's shape templates come from the dedicated +-1% BES samples, the 1e-5 momentum-scale and the 2 MeV sqrt(s) variations (run book, Systematics).
- **+ JES/JER**: `xsec` of the hadronic JES/JER run dirs (a 1% jet energy scale and an additional 1% jet energy smearing profiled as shape nuisances on every process of the hadronic card; Z(qq)H and the combination only). - Configurations (Sec. 6): MVA-based = BDT categorization and, for Z(qq)H, the 2D m_recoil x m_jj fit; cut-based = "each final state is fitted in a single region, and the hadronic final state is fitted using only the recoil mass distribution" (the same cards with one bin: `makeWS_recoil_2cat.py --single-region`, `makeWS_2D_qq.py --recoil-only`; the leptonic single region = loBDT + hiBDT = the paper's Sec. 4.2 selection, the hadronic one = stage2 sel_Baseline_nomva, m_recoil projection).
